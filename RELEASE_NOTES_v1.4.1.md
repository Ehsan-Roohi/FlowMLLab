# FlowMLLab v1.4.1

An author-requested maintenance release consolidating stabilization and the
Week 10.1 ab initio collision DeepONet reading companion. Existing v1.4.0
archives and DOI remain unchanged.

## Week 10.1 research companion

- Add a lecture reading note and four colored cylinder-field comparisons:
  translational temperature, Mach number, pressure and normalized density.
- Attribute the research case to Roohi, Shoja-sani and Stefanov,
  *Physics of Fluids* 38, 057123 (2026),
  [10.1063/5.0328463](https://doi.org/10.1063/5.0328463).
- Identify the author-supplied July DeepONet package as distinct from the
  original article's MLP and the Week 7.1 Fusion-DeepONet problem.
- Preserve exact/deep outputs 98/95 and their different physical times and
  sampling windows. No synchronized error map or speedup is claimed.
- Include verified source hashes, reproducible solid-masked plotting code,
  high-resolution figures and explicit dataset/source reuse boundaries.
- Keep all earlier gallery figures and place the new companion in course order.

## Stabilization since v1.4.0

- Scientific documentation landing page and executed notebook previews.
- Per-case research provenance and transparent AI-assistance disclosure.

## Validation and release contents

The tree retains 25 notebooks, 12 lectures (11 PDFs), plus the Markdown
Week 10.1 reading companion. It introduces no new training notebook.
Publication is gated by package tests, scientific smoke validation, full
course QA, and wheel/source-distribution checks. The GitHub release includes
the distributions, companion note and contour overview. The source archive
contains the full retained course tree; the large private research ZIP and
original checkpoint are not redistributed.
