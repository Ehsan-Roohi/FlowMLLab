# Week 7: cylinder mechanics and forecasting

Start with [the original LBM lab](W7_Lattice_Boltzmann_Cylinder_Student.ipynb)
and [its lecture](../../lectures/week07_cylinder_lbm_neural_surrogate.pdf).

The development [modal forecasting companion](W7_Lab2_Modal_Forecasting.ipynb)
compares original projected DMD with a fresh MLP, persistence and POD floors
on real, previously published author LBM fields. Read its
[lecture](../../lectures/week07_modal_forecasting.pdf) and
[protocol/results](../../results/modal_labs/README.md).

The companion predicts future time in Re110; it does not claim new-Re transfer,
grid-independent CFD, noise-robust DMD, or vortex segmentation. It is separate
from Week 7.1's hypersonic DSMC cylinder. Main-branch Colab links activate after
merge; local Run All is the development entry point.
