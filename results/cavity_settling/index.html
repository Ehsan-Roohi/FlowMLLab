When has a cavity flow settled?

Final results from 22 numerical configurations, seven long trajectories and three tighter-coupling confirmation branches. The companion notebook makes the stationarity audit and all 16 matched controls reproducible from saved data.

Re = 100, 400 and 1000 pass fixed-grid temporal stationarity checks. The higher-Re endpoints remain unresolved. All runs have finished, but this does not establish that every case is steady or spatially converged.
