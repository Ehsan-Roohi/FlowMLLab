# Week 7.4 retained diverse-wake pretraining evidence

This folder is generated by `qa/run_week07_4_protocol.py`.  It tests whether
masked pretraining on many unlabelled wake trajectories makes instantaneous
lift decoding more label-efficient on a new trajectory.

The split is trajectory-wise: 11 Reynolds cases for pretraining and development,
Re = 105 for every hyperparameter choice, and Re = 95, 115, 125 and 135 for the
final test.  For each test trajectory, labels may come only from frames `[0,150)`;
frames `[180,251)` are scored.  The test cases are never used to choose the
encoder, POD rank or ridge strength.

Mean lift-decoding NRMSE (%) across four unseen trajectories (three labelled-frame
draws within each trajectory):

| k labels | Pretrained encoder + ridge | Random encoder + ridge | POD-32 + ridge |
| ---: | ---: | ---: | ---: |
| 2 | 99.44 | 120.91 | 81.06 |
| 4 | 68.46 | 89.45 | 50.44 |
| 8 | 35.96 | 49.89 | 30.65 |
| 16 | 22.59 | 35.35 | 19.24 |
| 32 | 12.31 | 26.43 | 10.84 |
| 64 | 6.05 | 19.72 | 7.00 |
| 128 | 4.22 | 16.02 | 4.73 |

The pretrained representation beats the identical random encoder at every k.
At k = 32 it is already more accurate than the random encoder at k = 128, so
the accuracy is better with one-quarter as many target labels. POD remains stronger through
k = 32; the pretrained representation crosses it at k = 64 and 128.  This is
the bounded success claim.  It does not establish performance outside these
quick LBM trajectories or for high-fidelity experimental wakes.

- `metrics.json`: frozen protocol, per-trajectory records, selected alphas,
  environment and data hashes.
- `label_efficiency.png`: the principal result and homepage figure.
- `re115_lift_decoding.png`: one untouched trajectory at k = 32.
- `pretraining_loss.png`: validation-only stopping evidence.

## Audit qualifications

Ridge selection additionally uses 2761 development and 251 validation lift labels.
The reported k counts target labels only. One encoder initialization is retained;
the three seeds resample target labels, not pretraining. POD rank 32 is fixed,
not selected from a rank sweep. One trajectory per Reynolds number varies its
initial perturbation together with Reynolds number, so initial-condition
generalization is not isolated. Mean improvements do not establish statistical
significance or superiority on every trajectory. These test cases are now
inspected retained evidence and cannot be reused for future model selection.

At k=128, pretrained beats POD-32 on Re95, Re125 and Re135; POD-32 remains better on Re115 (3.83% versus 4.24%). The aggregate crossing must not be read as a win on every target.
