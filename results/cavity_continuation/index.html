A common endpoint is not a steady-state test. These are finite-resolution, two-dimensional trajectories. A flow that looks quiet may still change, and high-Re fields remain mesh-sensitive. Fixed-grid stationarity, tighter-coupling confirmation and timestep/mesh accuracy require separate evidence. No panel is labelled steady merely because its movie reaches t⋆ = 100.

Black curves are instantaneous streamlines, not particle paths. Pressure has zero area mean. Every panel uses the same domain and fixed field scales for the entire interval. The limits include all retained native extrema, including the finer-cadence original startup frames; no field is clipped or smoothed. The 8-frame/s playback rate changes viewing speed, not the physical timestamps.
