# Nozzle article reference figure

`nozzle_throat_X030_profiles.svg` is Figure 20 from Roohi and Mahdavi,
*Shock-Centered Low-Rank Structure and Neural-Operator Representation of
Rarefied Micro-Nozzle Flows* (2026). The source article is available at
[arXiv:2605.12723](https://arxiv.org/abs/2605.12723) and
[doi:10.1063/5.0343101](https://doi.org/10.1063/5.0343101) under CC BY 4.0.

`nozzle_throat_X030_profiles.png` is a 1800-pixel-wide raster conversion of the
same SVG for the Week-9 PDF. No curves or labels were changed. This figure is a
literature reference; the FlowMLLab-generated back-pressure results are stored
separately in `../nozzle_flowmllab/`.
