What was checked

01 / Analytic motion

Exact free flight and isolated collisions with fixed and rotating walls. Expected velocity is derived from wall-relative motion at the prescribed impact time.

02 / Conservation

A fixed elastic container conserves K + mgy. A uniformly rotating elastic container with zero gravity conserves K − ωL. No assumption of constant laboratory energy is made for a moving wall.

03 / Geometry

An external half-plane calculation checks the full ball radius against all six walls at 2400 samples per simulated second.

Scope, provenance, and reproduction

Labels Extra High, Medium, and Light were reported by the user. The model identity and reasoning settings were not independently verified. Earlier outputs and tests were visible to later generations; the first retained implementation also received iterative corrections. No new model generations were requested for this audit.

The audit was written by the same assistant after the outputs were retained. “Independent” means its measurements and analytic expectations do not use solver diagnostics; it is not a blinded third-party evaluation. Original fragments and checksums are retained. Exact physics scripts are extracted without changing their equations.

From this directory, run node tests/audit.cjs (Node.js 18 or later). Read the full report, prompt record, and checksums. This is a deterministic audit of three implementations, not evidence about output variability, model-wide reliability, or statistical significance.
