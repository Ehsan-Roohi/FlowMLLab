From research cases to teaching experiments

The article-linked DSMC cases originate in earlier research by Ehsan Roohi and collaborators. FlowMLLab brings selected data into a teaching setting; its new loaders, baselines and classroom models are distinct from the original research solvers and published neural models.

Study and reproduce

Follow Week 1 through Week 13, including incremental labs 1.1, 2.1, 4.1, 4.2, 7.1 and 7.2 and the Week 10.1 scattering lab and reading companion. Each week has its own learning material.

  • Notebooks and Colab launch links
  • Executed Week 10.1: scattering and surrogate audit
  • Executed Week 7.1: cylinder MLP and interpolation
  • Executed Week 9 Lab 2: nozzle baselines and registration
  • Lecture notes
  • Week 4.2: PINNs and the lid-driven cavity — continuous-text notes, hard boundary constraints and an attributed reading of McDevitt's code; no new high-Re training claim.
  • Week 10.1 reading companion: ab initio collision DeepONet and colored cylinder fields — related to Roohi et al., Physics of Fluids 38, 057123 (2026); different output times, qualitative comparison only.
  • Week 11: shock and vortex identification — CPU controls plus six real airfoil/cylinder model runs; no new CFD or human-validated accuracy.
  • Week 12: DSMC moment reconstruction — synthetic controls, fresh CPU Noise2Noise-style training on real DSMC observations, and a separate historical research audit. Includes whole-seed splits, simple baselines and retained failures; no new DSMC simulation.
  • Week 13: rectangular-cavity PINN research audit — retained float64 A100 runs across Reynolds number and depth, Adam-to-SSBroyden2 histories, exact walls, CFD gates for square cases and explicit no-field-reference limits for deep cases.
  • Retained results, reproduction commands and known defects

Retained results are evidence, not notebook scratch space. Executing a teaching example does not by itself reproduce a paper's accuracy.

Release scope and course extensions

Version 1.5.0 consolidates 33 notebooks and 19 PDF lectures through Week 13. It includes the incremental scientific-software, state-estimation and scattering labs, DSMC reconstruction and retained Unity cavity-PINN evidence.

The historical v1.4.0 and v1.4.1 archives retain their original DOI records. See the v1.5.0 release notes for included evidence and its limits.

Nozzle symmetry/export defects remain unresolved in the source data. The cylinder teaching dataset is now CC BY 4.0 with Ehsan Roohi's authorization; see its data license. Licensing alone does not establish scientific readiness for external benchmark adoption.

Scientific interpretation and publication decisions remain the authors' responsibility. See the repository for data-specific attribution and AI-assistance disclosure.
