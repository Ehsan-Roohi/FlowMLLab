CFD POD-DeepONet

Scales, model split and scientific limits

Every Reynolds number uses the same colour limits for a given quantity, and both methods share spatial bounds. Speed uses a linear scale. Pressure uses a symmetric-log scale, linear within ±0.02; vorticity is linear within ±1. Both retain the full extrema. Signed differences use a separate symmetric linear scale shared across all seven cases.

Pressure is recovered from steady momentum gradients with a separate zero-mean gauge for each interior field. It is not an independently validated pressure solution. Vorticity uses the same centred velocity derivatives for both methods. Pressure and vorticity are shown only at the 511 × 511 interior nodes, without extrapolated wall values.

The frozen network is the mean of three hybrid_2 POD-DeepONet fits (seeds 7, 23, 61). Training Re: 100, 250, 500, 750, 1,000, 2,500, 4,000, 5,000, 6,000, 9,000 and 10,000. Validation Re: 400 and 6,500. Held-out tests: 3,200 and 7,500. Ghia points were not used in fitting. Switching cases displays saved results; it does not train a network or solve CFD.

This finite-grid steady two-dimensional reference does not establish mesh independence or physical stability at high Re. Inspect mesh and time sensitivity and model evaluation and residuals.

Week 15 extension

Do larger networks predict two-step flow better?

Compare six enlarged geometry-aware models against retained CFD, then separate whole-field accuracy from reverse-flow topology. In the three completed matched controls, enlargement reduced mean velocity error by 18.82% for geometry-aware DeepONet and 12.16% for Geom-DeepONet; SMART's error increased by 7.26%. Each configuration has one training seed.
